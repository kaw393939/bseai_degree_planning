NY Fed – recent college grads (major-specific)
- Computer Science (recent grads, age 22–27): 6.1% unemployment in the latest published “by major” table (data year 2023; released Feb 2025). Underemployment for CS: 16.5%. The NY Fed updates the by‑major table annually each February; the 2024 major-by-major figures are due with the Feb 2026 update. - Overall recent grads: unemployment rose to about 5.7% in 2025 Q4 (highest since 2020); underemployment 42.5%. ([newyorkfed.org](https://www.newyorkfed.org/research/college-labor-market))

BLS – jobless rates for tech occupational group (CPS)

  - 2024: mostly 2–4% month to month (e.g., 2.0% in Dec 2024). ([ycharts.com](https://ycharts.com/indicators/us_unemployment_rate_experienced_as_computer_and_mathematical_occupations_unadjusted?utm_source=openai))

  - 2026 YTD: 3.6% in Jan 2026. Note: occupation-specific rates are for workers with prior experience in that occupation (“experienced unemployed”). ([ycharts.com](https://ycharts.com/indicators/us_unemployment_rate_experienced_as_computer_and_mathematical_occupations_unadjusted?utm_source=openai))

Software developer job market: postings, roles, and layoffs
- Programming vs. developer roles: BLS data show a steep drop in “computer programmer” employment (down >25% over two years through early 2025), while “software developers” held roughly steady—reflecting automation of routine coding and a shift toward broader developer roles. ([washingtonpost.com](https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2025/03/14/programming-jobs-lost-artificial-intelligence/?utm_source=openai))
- Job postings: Software development postings fell to a multi‑year low by early 2025 (Indeed/Pragmatic Engineer analyses). ([tech.slashdot.org](https://tech.slashdot.org/story/25/02/21/111216/software-engineering-job-openings-hit-five-year-low?utm_source=openai))
- Layoffs: Tech layoffs peaked in 2023, continued through 2024–2025, and are still occurring in early 2026 (Layoffs.fyi tallies reported by outlets). Examples: ~100k tech layoffs in 2025; ~25k globally in Jan 2026 alone. ([computerworld.com](https://www.computerworld.com/article/3816579/tech-layoffs-this-year-a-timeline.html?utm_source=openai))
- Despite near‑term softness, BLS projections (2024–2034) still expect:
  - Computer & mathematical occupations: +10.1% employment. 
  - Software developers: +15.8% and +267,700 jobs added (second‑largest numeric gain among all occupations). ([bls.gov](https://www.bls.gov/opub/mlr/2026/article/industry-and-occupational-employment-projections-overview.htm?utm_source=openai))
