Here’s a concise brief on Trump administration AI policy and action items (2025–2026), with pointers to OSTP, AI.gov, education/workforce, and “Stargate.”

Top executive actions and federal strategy
- Executive Order: Removing Barriers to American Leadership in Artificial Intelligence (signed January 23, 2025). Revokes Biden’s 2023 AI order, directs a government‑wide review to suspend/rescind conflicting actions, and requires an AI Action Plan within 180 days; also instructs OMB to revise key AI memos. Lead roles include the Assistant to the President for Science & Technology (OSTP), the Special Advisor for AI & Crypto, and the National Security Advisor. ([whitehouse.gov](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/01/removing-barriers-to-american-leadership-in-artificial-intelligence/?utm_source=openai))
- AI Action Plan released: “Winning the AI Race: America’s AI Action Plan” (July 23, 2025). Lays out 90+ actions under three pillars: Accelerating Innovation; Building American AI Infrastructure; and Leading in International Diplomacy & Security. Examples include expediting permits for data centers/semiconductor fabs; coordinated “full‑stack” AI export packages; and procurement rules to ensure “objective,” ideology‑free LLMs. ([whitehouse.gov](https://www.whitehouse.gov/articles/2025/07/white-house-unveils-americas-ai-action-plan/?utm_source=openai))
- OSTP public input: OSTP issued an RFI for the Action Plan (February 25, 2025) and reported substantial public comment. ([whitehouse.gov](https://www.whitehouse.gov/briefings-statements/2025/02/public-comment-invited-on-artificial-intelligence-action-plan/?utm_source=openai))
- OMB/government use of AI: Subsequent policy steers agencies toward “unbiased” AI and updates procurement expectations; agencies were told to update internal policies by March 11, 2026. ([govexec.com](https://www.govexec.com/technology/2025/12/white-house-instructs-agencies-stop-using-biased-ai/410138/?utm_source=openai))
- AI.gov relaunch: The National AI Initiative site (AI.gov) now aggregates the administration’s AI agenda, listing the above EOs and fact sheets, plus ongoing initiatives and memos. ([ai.gov](https://www.ai.gov/))

Education and workforce (OSTP‑led Task Force)
- Executive Order: Advancing Artificial Intelligence Education for American Youth (April 23, 2025). Creates a White House Task Force on AI Education (chaired by OSTP) and directs actions by Education, Labor, NSF, USDA, and others to: integrate AI into K‑12; expand teacher training; stand up a Presidential AI Challenge; and grow AI‑related Registered Apprenticeships and WIOA‑supported training. ([whitehouse.gov](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/04/advancing-artificial-intelligence-education-for-american-youth/?utm_source=openai))
- AI.gov education hub: Summarizes Task Force membership and deliverables (K‑12 resources, educator PD, lifelong learning). ([ai.gov](https://www.ai.gov/initiatives/education?utm_source=openai))
- Presidential AI Challenge: Competition for students/educators; 2026 registration cycle has closed. ([ai.gov](https://www.ai.gov/initiatives/presidential-challenge?utm_source=openai))
- Public‑private commitments: The White House announced pledges from 60+ organizations to support AI education (June 30, 2025), alongside later updates highlighting additional commitments. ([whitehouse.gov](https://www.whitehouse.gov/articles/2025/06/60-organizations-sign-white-house-pledge-to-support-americas-youth-and-invest-in-ai-education/?utm_source=openai))

“Stargate” and AI infrastructure
- OpenAI and partners announced the Stargate Project on January 21, 2025—an intended multi‑hundred‑billion‑dollar U.S. AI infrastructure buildout (with an initial $100B tranche and early sites in Texas). This squares with the Action Plan’s emphasis on accelerating data center and semiconductor permitting and workforce pipelines (e.g., electricians/HVAC). ([openai.com](https://openai.com/index/announcing-the-stargate-project/?utm_source=openai))
