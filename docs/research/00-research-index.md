## Citation Standards

1. Every statistic includes **(source, date)** in parentheses
2. URLs are hyperlinked for verification
3. Where numbers come from vendor self-reports vs independent evaluation, that distinction is noted
4. Where third-party aggregators are used instead of official sources, that is flagged
5. Salary ranges use the consolidated canonical set from `06-salary-data.md`

## Source Reliability Tiers

- **Tier 1 (highest):** Peer-reviewed papers (Nature, arXiv with citations), official government data (BLS, NY Fed), official benchmark leaderboards (swebench.com, lastexam.ai, arcprize.org)
- **Tier 2:** Major consulting firms (McKinsey, Goldman Sachs, Gartner, Deloitte, Accenture), vendor model cards with methodology (Anthropic, OpenAI, DeepMind)
- **Tier 3:** Reputable journalism (Fortune, Bloomberg, WSJ, TechCrunch), third-party trackers (levels.fyi, CompTIA, CB Insights)
- **Tier 4 (use with caveat):** Self-reported vendor benchmarks without independent replication, blog posts, social media claims
