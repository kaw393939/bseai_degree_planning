## Answer

Here’s a 2025–2026 snapshot comparing AI-focused undergraduate options across the schools you named. I’ve highlighted whether there’s a standalone AI major, what the curriculum emphasizes, and notable “enterprise/industry” elements.

Carnegie Mellon (CMU)
- Degree: Standalone B.S. in Artificial Intelligence (School of Computer Science). ([coursecatalog.web.cmu.edu](https://coursecatalog.web.cmu.edu/schools-colleges/schoolofcomputerscience/artificialintelligence/?utm_source=openai))
- Curriculum shape: Heavy CS/math/statistics core plus 7 dedicated AI courses, 1 ethics course, SCS electives, science/engineering, and humanities/arts; total ≈360 units. Undergraduate research thesis option (07-599) can count toward SCS electives. ([coursecatalog.web.cmu.edu](https://coursecatalog.web.cmu.edu/schools-colleges/schoolofcomputerscience/artificialintelligence/?utm_source=openai))
- Distinctives: Nation’s first B.S. in AI (launched in 2018) housed in SCS; strong research integration. ([ai.cmu.edu](https://ai.cmu.edu/index.php/curriculum?utm_source=openai))

Stanford
- Degree: No standalone AI B.S.; AI is a track within the B.S. in Computer Science. Symbolic Systems also offers AI-adjacent pathways. ([cs.stanford.edu](https://www.cs.stanford.edu/bachelors-compsci-tracks-overview?utm_source=openai))
- Curriculum shape: CS core (math, science, engineering fundamentals, CS core) plus one depth “track.” Tracks are at least 25 units/7 classes; AI track includes courses like CS221 (AI: Principles & Techniques) with additional related electives; senior project required. ([cs.stanford.edu](https://www.cs.stanford.edu/bachelors-compsci-tracks-overview?utm_source=openai))
- Distinctives: Broad CS degree with flexible AI depth; access to SAIL ecosystem. ([ai.stanford.edu](https://ai.stanford.edu/?utm_source=openai))

Georgia Tech (GT)
- Degree: No standalone AI B.S.; AI is the “Intelligence” Thread within the B.S. in Computer Science (students pick two Threads, e.g., Theory & Intelligence). ([catalog.gatech.edu](https://catalog.gatech.edu/programs/computer-science-bs/?utm_source=openai))
- Curriculum shape (2025–2026 catalog example): CS 3600 (Intro to AI) in-thread; advanced math (e.g., MATH 3406); ethics options include CS 4003 (AI, Ethics, and Society); junior design/capstone; specific menus vary by thread pair (e.g., Theory & Intelligence). ([catalog.gatech.edu](https://catalog.gatech.edu/programs/theory-intelligence-computer-science-bs/?utm_source=openai))
- Distinctives: “Threads” model for tailored depth; campus AI Makerspace/supercomputer access integrated into undergrad courses. ([ece.gatech.edu](https://ece.gatech.edu/news/2025/05/ai-makerspace-turns-one-students-charting-course?utm_source=openai))

Purdue
- Degree: Standalone B.S. in Artificial Intelligence (Department of Computer Science). Separate B.A. in AI also exists in Liberal Arts. ([cs.purdue.edu](https://www.cs.purdue.edu/undergraduate/curriculum/bachelor-ai.html?utm_source=openai))
- Curriculum shape (CS B.S. in AI): CS fundamentals plus AI/ML, psychology (PSY 12000), philosophy/ethics selective, and upper-division CS selectives (e.g., NLP, HCI, data analytics); C-or-better in major courses. Page last updated July 15, 2025. ([cs.purdue.edu](https://www.cs.purdue.edu/undergraduate/curriculum/bachelor-ai.html?utm_source=openai))
- Distinctives: University-wide “AI working competency” graduation requirement begins with the Fall 2026 entering class (discipline-tailored). ([purdue.edu](https://www.purdue.edu/newsroom/2025/Q4/purdue-unveils-comprehensive-ai-strategy-trustees-approve-ai-working-competency-graduation-requirement/?utm_source=openai))

Arizona State University (ASU)
- Degree: Standalone B.S. in Artificial Intelligence in Business (W. P. Carey). No standalone technical AI B.S. in SCAI; technical routes are BS in CS (with concentrations like cybersecurity or software engineering), plus AI-focused grad pathways. ([degrees.apps.asu.edu](https://degrees.apps.asu.edu/bachelors/major/ASU00/BAAIBBS/artificial-intelligence-in-business?utm_source=openai))
- Curriculum shape (AI in Business B.S.): 120 credits blending business core with data analysis, ML, big data, and hands-on tools (e.g., OpenAI, AWS). On-ground/online; Polytechnic campus availability slated for Fall 2026. ([degrees.apps.asu.edu](https://degrees.apps.asu.edu/bachelors/major/ASU00/BAAIBBS/artificial-intelligence-in-business?utm_source=openai))
- Distinctives: Large, enterprise-oriented AI initiative and OpenAI partnership supporting campus use of ChatGPT Enterprise and AI projects. ([ai.asu.edu](https://ai.asu.edu/?utm_source=openai))
