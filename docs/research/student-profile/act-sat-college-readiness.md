Here are the ACT College Readiness benchmarks and how the Class of 2024 performed nationally.

Benchmarks (the scores that indicate readiness for first‑year, credit‑bearing college courses)
- English: 18
- Reading: 22
- Math: 22
- Science: 23
- ELA (English + Reading + Writing): 20
- STEM (Math + Science): 26. These benchmarks correspond to about a 50% chance of earning a B or better and ~75% chance of earning a C or better in the related course. ([act.org](https://www.act.org/content/act/en/college-and-career-readiness/benchmarks.html?utm_source=openai))

Class of 2024 national snapshot (released October 2024)
- Average ACT composite: 19.4
- Share meeting each benchmark: English 51%, Math 29%, Reading 40%, Science 30%
- Met all four core benchmarks: 20%
- Met three or four benchmarks: 30%; 57% met at least one benchmark
- Tested students: 1,374,791 (about 36% of graduates) ([act.org](https://www.act.org/content/dam/act/unsecured/documents/2024-act-national-graduating-class-profile-report.pdf))
