## Answer

Here’s a concise rollup of NAEP (The Nation’s Report Card) reading results and trends tied to the years you listed, with emphasis on proficiency levels, below-Basic shares, and pandemic-era learning loss.

What happened nationally
- Main NAEP, grades 4 and 8 (tested spring 2019 and spring 2024; 2021 was postponed to 2022)
  - Scores fell from 2019 to 2022, then fell again from 2022 to 2024. In 2024, average reading dropped a further 2 points at both grades vs 2022; no state posted gains, and the share of students below NAEP Basic hit record or near‑record highs. ([nagb.gov](https://www.nagb.gov/news-and-events/news-releases/2022/report-card-score-decline-4th-8th-grade-math-reading.html?utm_source=openai))
- Grade 12 (tested spring 2019 and spring 2024)
  - Average reading fell 3 points from 2019 to 2024, reaching the lowest 12th‑grade average on record; 35% were at/above Proficient and 32% were below Basic in 2024. ([nagb.gov](https://www.nagb.gov/news-and-events/news-releases/2025/declines-in-8th-grade-science-and-12th-grade-math-and-reading.html))
- Long-Term Trend (LTT, age-based)
  - Age 9 (winter 2022): reading fell 5 points vs 2020—the largest LTT reading drop since 1990. Age 13 (fall 2023): reading fell 4 points vs 2020, with declines across the score distribution and the lowest “reads for fun almost every day” on record (14%). ([nationsreportcard.gov](https://www.nationsreportcard.gov/highlights/ltt/2022/?utm_source=openai))
- Big picture: NAEP points to a decade of declines that began pre‑pandemic and then worsened through COVID disruptions. ([nagb.gov](https://www.nagb.gov/naep-results/reading.html?utm_source=openai))

By grade: proficiency and below‑Basic snapshots
- 4th grade
  - At/above Proficient: 35% (2019), 33% (2022), 31% (2024). ([nationsreportcard.gov](https://www.nationsreportcard.gov/reading/nation/achievement/))
  - Below Basic: 34% (2019), 37% (2022), about 40% (2024; largest since 2002). ([prnewswire.com](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/mathematics-and-reading-scores-of-fourth--and-eighth-graders-declined-in-most-states-during-pandemic-nations-report-card-shows-301656605.html?utm_source=openai))
- 8th grade
  - At/above Proficient: 31% (2022) → ~30% (2024). ([edweek.org](https://www.edweek.org/teaching-learning/5-things-to-know-about-the-slide-in-reading-achievement-on-naep/2022/10?utm_source=openai))
  - Below Basic: 27% (2019) → 30% (2022) → about one‑third (2024; largest ever). - 12th grade
  - At/above Proficient: 37% (2019) → 35% (2024). Below Basic: 30% (2019) → 32% (2024; largest ever share below Basic at grade 12). ([nagb.gov](https://www.nagb.gov/news-and-events/news-releases/2025/declines-in-8th-grade-science-and-12th-grade-math-and-reading.html))

Timing notes to avoid confusion about “2024/2025”

The 2023 LTT (age 13) reflects testing in fall 2022–23. ([nagb.gov](https://www.nagb.gov/news-and-events/news-releases/2024/naep-to-be-released-jan-29-2025.html?utm_source=openai))

What this says about pandemic learning loss and trends
- Reading achievement had been drifting down since roughly the mid‑2010s; COVID‑era disruptions deepened those declines. The sharpest drops concentrated among lower‑performing students, driving record shares below NAEP Basic in 2024 at grades 4, 8, and 12. ([nagb.gov](https://www.nagb.gov/naep-results/reading.html?utm_source=openai))
