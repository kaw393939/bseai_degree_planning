CB Insights (global VC/venture funding to private AI companies)

Stanford HAI — AI Index 2025 (private investment; broader than pure VC)
- Coverage: through full-year 2024 (the 2025 report summarizes 2024 data; 2025 totals will appear in the next AI Index). ([hai.stanford.edu](https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2025-ai-index-report?u=))
- 2024 global private AI investment: $150.79B; total corporate AI investment (private investment + M&A + minority stakes + IPOs): $252.33B. Generative AI private investment: $33.94B. ([hai.stanford.edu](https://hai.stanford.edu/assets/files/hai_ai-index-report-2025_chapter4_final.pdf))
- 2024 by country (private investment): US $109.08B; China $9.29B; UK $4.52B. ([hai.stanford.edu](https://hai.stanford.edu/assets/files/hai_ai-index-report-2025_chapter4_final.pdf))

Quick definitions (why the numbers differ)
- CB Insights reports venture funding to private AI companies (equity rounds; includes CVC participation), a narrower VC lens. ([cbinsights.com](https://www.cbinsights.com/research/report/ai-trends-2024/?utm_source=openai))
- Stanford HAI’s “private investment” (via Quid/PitchBook data) is broader: it tracks private investment and also breaks out M&A, minority stakes, and public offerings when discussing “corporate AI investment.” That’s why Stanford’s totals (e.g., $150.79B private; $252.33B corporate) are higher than CB Insights’ VC-only figure. ([hai.stanford.edu](https://hai.stanford.edu/assets/files/hai_ai-index-report-2025_chapter4_final.pdf))
