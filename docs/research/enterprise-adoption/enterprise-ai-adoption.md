## Answer

Here are the clearest “in production/deployed” enterprise AI adoption datapoints from Gartner and Deloitte, by year. Note: the two firms often measure different things (org-level deployment vs. share of projects fully in production), so I’m keeping their original definitions.

2024
- Gartner (Q4 2023 survey; released May 7, 2024): 29% of organizations said they had deployed and were using generative AI; on average, only 48% of AI projects make it into production, and the median time from prototype to production is about 8 months. ([gartner.com](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2024-05-07-gartner-survey-finds-generative-ai-is-now-the-most-frequently-deployed-ai-solution-in-organizations))
- Deloitte (2024 series; Q3/Q4 readouts): A “large majority” (about 68%) reported that 30% or fewer of their GenAI experiments had been fully scaled/put into production, underscoring limited production penetration in 2024. ([www2.deloitte.com](https://www2.deloitte.com/us/en/pages/about-deloitte/articles/press-releases/state-of-generative-ai-Q3.html?utm_source=openai))

2025
- Gartner (Q4 2024 survey; released June 30, 2025): Among high–AI‑maturity organizations, 45% keep AI initiatives running in production three years or more (vs. 20% for low maturity) — a measure of production durability rather than headline adoption. ([gartner.com](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-06-30-gartner-survey-finds-forty-five-percent-of-organizations-with-high-artificial-intelligence-maturity-keep-artificial-intelligence-projects-operational-for-at-least-three-years?utm_source=openai))
- Deloitte (fielded Aug–Sep 2025; published as the 2026 “State of AI in the Enterprise”): 25% of respondents said their organization had already moved 40%+ of AI experiments into production; 54% expected to reach that level within 3–6 months. ([deloitte.com](https://www.deloitte.com/cz-sk/en/services/consulting/research/the-state-of-ai-in-the-enterprise.html))

2026
- Gartner (forecast made Oct 2023 for 2026): By 2026, over 80% of enterprises will have used GenAI APIs and/or deployed GenAI‑enabled applications in production (prediction, not a survey reading). ([gartner.com](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2023-10-16-gartner-identifies-the-top-10-strategic-technology-trends-for-2024?utm_source=openai))
- Deloitte (same 2026 report, based on Aug–Sep 2025 fieldwork): expectation that the share of companies with ≥40% of AI projects in production would roughly double to 54% within six months of the survey window (i.e., into early 2026). ([deloitte.com](https://www.deloitte.com/cz-sk/en/services/consulting/research/the-state-of-ai-in-the-enterprise.html))
