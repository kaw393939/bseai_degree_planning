## 4. The Story for the Presentation

**Three facts that frame the enterprise case:**

1. **The money is flowing:** $225.8B in private AI funding in 2025. Big Tech committing $500B+ in 2026 capex alone. $2.53T total AI spending projected for 2026.
2. **But adoption is stuck:** Only 29% of organizations have deployed GenAI (Gartner). Only 25% have ≥40% of experiments in production (Deloitte). Only 8–16% are scaling enterprise-wide (Accenture).
3. **The bottleneck isn't capability — it's people:** AI models can code at 80%+ on SWE-bench. But enterprises need people who can evaluate, deploy, govern, and scale AI systems in production environments with real data, real stakeholders, and real risk.

That bottleneck is the BS in Enterprise AI.
