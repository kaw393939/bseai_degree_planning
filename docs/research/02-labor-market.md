## 3. Job Posting Trends

### What's Declining

- **"Computer Programmers" (narrow BLS category):** Down **25%+ in 2 years**. Generative AI and consolidation hit routine coding hardest. ([Washington Post / BLS](https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2025/03/14/programming-jobs-lost-artificial-intelligence/))
- **Software development postings:** Down **~51% YoY** by Oct 2023; remained weak through 2024. ([Indeed Hiring Lab](https://www.hiringlab.org/2023/11/15/indeeds-2024-us-jobs-hiring-trends-report/))
- Posted wages barely grew: +0.7% YoY in Dec 2024 for software dev. ([Indeed Hiring Lab](https://www.hiringlab.org/2023/11/15/indeeds-2024-us-jobs-hiring-trends-report/))

### What's Growing

- **AI-skill postings:** ~125,000 in May 2025. AI is the bright spot even as broader tech hiring cooled. ([CompTIA](https://www.comptia.org/en-us/resources/research/state-of-the-tech-workforce-2025/))
- By late 2025, **4.2% of all U.S. postings** mentioned AI; **45% of data & analytics postings** did. ([Indeed Hiring Lab, Jan 2026](https://www.hiringlab.org/2026/01/22/january-labor-market-update-jobs-mentioning-ai-are-growing-amid-broader-hiring-weakness/))

## 7. The Two-Track Labor Market Story

**For the presentation, the data tells a clear story:**

1. **Traditional programming jobs are declining** — down 25%+ in 2 years, postings down 51%, entry-level CS unemployment at 6.1%
2. **AI-integrated roles are surging** — AI postings +87% in NYC, data scientists +34% BLS projection, 4.2% of all U.S. job postings now mention AI
3. **The gap is the opportunity** — students who graduate with only traditional CS skills face a contracting market; students who graduate with enterprise AI skills enter an expanding one
4. **NJIT is perfectly positioned** — #1 AI job market (NYC) is 30 minutes away, NJ AI Hub launching, top employers already recruit NJIT grads
