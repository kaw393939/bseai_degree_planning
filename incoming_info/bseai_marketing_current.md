Information Technology
Enter NJIT's vision for the Applied Enterprise AI program. We're not creating another technical degree; we're building a new kind of professional. One who combines deep technical expertise with professional skills and values that are essential for enterprise technology success.

Objectives
Rooted in the foundations of traditional Information Systems, our Bachelor of Science in Enterprise AI brings a modern touch, equipping students with the skills to design, develop, and deploy next-generation smart information systems. By integrating core principles of system analysis and data management with cutting-edge AI and machine learning technology, this program prepares you to create intelligent technologies that anticipate needs, automate processes, and enhance user experiences.

You Will Be Prepared to:

Design, implement and fine-tune machine learning and LLM models to solve business problems 
Architect enterprise solutions that scale with AI capabilities
Apply responsible and ethical AI principles in system development and deployment 
Lead teams through complex implementations
Communicate effectively with stakeholders at all levels. 
Core Alignments Between IS and Applied Enterprise AI
Fundamental Principles

Business-Technology Integration
IS Foundation: Bridge between business needs and technical solutions
AI Application: Extends this bridge to include AI capabilities
Alignment: Maintains focus on business value creation
System Implementation
IS Foundation: Focus on successful technology deployment
AI Application: Applies same principles to AI system implementation
Alignment: Shares emphasis on practical application
Data Management 
IS Foundation: Structured data handling and database design
AI Application: Enhanced data processing and ML model management
Alignment: Builds on established data handling principles
Enterprise Integration
IS Foundation: System integration and enterprise architecture
AI Application: AI service integration and enterprise AI architecture
Alignment: Maintains enterprise-wide perspective
