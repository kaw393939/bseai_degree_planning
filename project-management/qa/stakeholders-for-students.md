## Issue 2: "50–80% Premium" Calculation Depends on Which Range You Use (MEDIUM)

"Traditional IS graduates: $65K–$80K" vs "BS in EAI: $100K–$150K" → claimed "50–80% premium." Math check:
- $100K / $65K = 54% premium ✓
- $150K / $80K = 87.5% premium (rounds to ~88%, not 80%)

The "80%" claim requires $150K/$83K or $120K/$67K. The range "50–80%" is approximately right but imprecise at the upper bound. Not a dealbreaker, but a careful reader will check.

**Fix:** Either use "50–90%" or tighten to "50–75%" (using $100K–$130K ÷ $65K–$80K).
