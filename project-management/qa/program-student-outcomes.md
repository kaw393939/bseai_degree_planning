## Issue 1: 8 Capabilities vs 5 HE Outcomes — Mapping Unclear (CRITICAL)

The program markets 8 Human Edge capabilities everywhere (stripping thesis, overview, core-studio-spine, every syllabus). This page defines only 5 HE outcomes (HE-1 through HE-5). These are NOT the same constructs:

| HE Outcome | Closest Capability Match | Missing Capabilities |
|---|---|---|
| HE-1 Directed AI Orchestration | None directly — this is a meta-capability | — |
| HE-2 Judgment Under Uncertainty | Professional Judgment (IS 218) | — |
| HE-3 Failure Ownership | Resilience Thinking (IS 219) | — |
| HE-4 Epistemic Responsibility | Epistemic Humility (IS 331) | — |
| HE-5 Adaptive Re-Tooling | None directly — another meta-capability | — |

**Not explicitly represented as HE outcomes:**
- Disciplined Inquiry (IS 117)
- Translation (IS 118)
- Problem Finding (IS 265)
- Systems Thinking (IS 390)
- Accountable Leadership (IS 425)

These 5 capabilities feed into the 5 ABET general outcomes (SO-1 through SO-5) via the "Human Edge connection" notes, but the formal outcome structure creates a gap that an accreditor will notice: "You claim 8 unique capabilities but only formally assess 5 Human Edge outcomes."

**Fix:** Add an explicit bridging section: "How the 8 Studio Capabilities Map to the 5 HE Outcomes." Or better: expand to 8 HE outcomes matching the 8 capabilities.
