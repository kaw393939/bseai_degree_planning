## Issue 1: Mid-Career Salary Range Inflated (HIGH)

This page says "Mid-career AI salary range: $150K–$350K." Every other page on the site uses $150K–$250K for mid-career:
- `for-students.md`: "$150K–$250K" (mid-career, 5 years)
- `job-market.md`: "$150K–$250K" (Mid Level, 3–5 years)

The $350K upper bound on this page appears to blend the mid-career and senior ranges. This is the exact kind of inconsistency a skeptical dean will catch: "Why does the administrator page inflate the salary number?"

**Fix:** Change to "$150K–$250K" to match the evidence page, or use "$150K–$250K (senior roles: $200K–$350K+)" if you want to stretch.

## Issue 2: "Within Informatics" — Only Instance (LOW)

"Degree: BS in Enterprise AI (within Informatics)" — this is the only page on the entire site that positions the degree "within Informatics." Other pages say "Ying Wu College of Computing" or don't specify the department. Whether this is an error or additional specificity is unclear.

**Fix:** If the program administratively sits in the Informatics department, keep it but add the same label to the exec summary. If it's not confirmed, remove it.

## Issue 3: Revenue Projection — Good But Verify (LOW)

The revenue model ($1.62M Year 1 → $4.86M Year 3) assumes 30 → 120 students at $13,500/student. The per-student figure should be sourced (NJIT in-state tuition per credit × credits, or whatever the basis is). A provost will fact-check this number immediately.

**Fix:** Add a footnote: "Based on [X credits × $Y per credit hour] at current NJIT rates."
