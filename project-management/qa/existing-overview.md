## Issue 1: Lists 8 "What AI Cannot Automate" Items — Inconsistent with 8 Capabilities (MEDIUM)

The "What AI cannot automate" list has 8 bullet points:
1. Asking which question matters
2. Judging when the machine is wrong
3. Taking responsibility
4. Navigating ambiguity
5. Thinking in systems
6. Exercising moral reasoning
7. Creating meaning
8. Inspiring trust

But the 8 Human Edge *capabilities* mapped to courses are:
1. Disciplined Inquiry
2. Translation
3. Professional Judgment
4. Problem Finding
5. Resilience Thinking
6. Epistemic Humility
7. Systems Thinking
8. Accountable Leadership

These are two different lists. "Navigating ambiguity," "Creating meaning," and "Inspiring trust" appear in the overview's list but don't have corresponding studio capabilities. Conversely, "Translation," "Epistemic Humility," and "Problem Finding" are capabilities without direct matches in the overview's list.

This isn't necessarily wrong (the overview list is aspirational, the capabilities are operational), but a careful reader comparing them will notice the mismatch.

**Fix:** Either align the two lists or add a bridging sentence: "These aspirations are operationalized through eight studio capabilities, each taught and assessed in a specific course."
