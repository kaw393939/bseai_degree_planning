## Bureau of Labor Statistics Projections (2024–2034)

The BLS does not distinguish "AI engineer" as a separate occupation. AI-related roles are distributed across these categories:

### Software Developers (SOC 15-1252)

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| 2024 Median Annual Wage | $133,080 |
| 2024–2034 Projected Growth | +15% (much faster than average) |
| Projected New Jobs | ~287,900 |
| Total Employment (2024) | ~1.9 million |

Software developers with AI integration skills command premium salaries. The BLS notes that "demand for new applications and systems will continue to grow, especially for AI-driven software."

**Source:** [BLS OOH — Software Developers](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/software-developers.htm)

### Data Scientists (SOC 15-2051)

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| 2024 Median Annual Wage | $112,590 |
| 2024–2034 Projected Growth | +34% (much faster than average) |
| Status | Among the fastest-growing occupations |

The August 2025 BLS projections release specifically highlights AI adoption as a major demand driver for data science roles.

**Source:** [BLS OOH — Data Scientists](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/math/data-scientists.htm)

### Computer and Information Research Scientists (SOC 15-1221)

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| 2024 Median Annual Wage | $140,910 |
| 2024–2034 Projected Growth | +20% (much faster than average) |

This category captures AI research and advanced R&D roles — the highest-paid segment.

**Source:** [BLS OOH — Computer Research Scientists](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/computer-and-information-research-scientists.htm)

## Demand Indicators Beyond BLS

### Job Posting Trends

AI-related job postings have shown consistent growth:
- LinkedIn reports AI-related job postings grew **85%** in 2024–2025
- "AI engineer" has become the fastest-growing job title on major platforms
- Postings increasingly require **integration skills** (RAG, knowledge graphs, evaluation) rather than just model training

### Cross-Industry Demand

Every major industry sector is creating AI integration roles:

| Industry | AI Integration Need |
|----------|-------------------|
| **Healthcare** | Clinical AI systems, drug discovery, medical imaging, patient data management |
| **Finance** | Risk modeling, fraud detection, regulatory compliance, algorithmic trading |
| **Legal** | Document analysis, contract review, legal research, compliance monitoring |
| **Manufacturing** | Quality control, supply chain optimization, predictive maintenance |
| **Government** | Public service AI, policy analysis, security, administrative automation |
| **Education** | Adaptive learning, assessment, administrative efficiency |
| **Retail** | Recommendation systems, inventory optimization, customer service |

### The Integration Gap

The most critical hiring challenge reported by employers:

> "We can find people who can train models. We can find people who can write code. We cannot find people who can **build production AI systems that work with humans** — systems that are evaluated, observable, and accountable."

This is precisely what the BS in Enterprise AI teaches: the integration of AI with human judgment, evaluation, and accountability in production systems.

## Salary Comparison: EAI vs. Traditional IS Degree

| Metric | Traditional IS Graduate | BS in EAI Graduate (projected) |

The salary premium reflects the specialized integration skills that few programs teach.
