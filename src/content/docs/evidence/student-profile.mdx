# Who Walks in the Door

Before designing a curriculum, you must understand who will sit in the seats. This page presents an **evidence-based portrait** of students entering the BS in Enterprise AI (Fall 2026–2030), drawn from NAEP, ACT, OECD PISA, CDC, and peer-reviewed research.

The portrait is challenging. It is also the reason this program exists.

---

## 1. Academic Preparation: The National Picture

### Reading

| Grade | Year | At/Above Proficient | Below Basic | Source |
|-------|------|-------------------|-------------|--------|
| 4 | 2024 | 31% | ~40% (record high) | NAGB, Jan 2025 |
| 8 | 2024 | ~30% | ~33% (record) | NAGB, Jan 2025 |
| 12 | 2024 | 35% | 32% (record) | NAGB, Sep 2025 |

No state posted reading gains at grades 4 or 8 in 2024. Grade 12 reading was the **lowest 12th-grade average on record**. The share of students "below NAEP Basic" — unable to demonstrate even partial mastery of grade-level material — hit **record highs** at every tested grade. Only **14% of 13-year-olds** read for fun "almost every day" (NAEP Long-Term Trend, 2023) — the lowest ever measured.

Sources: [NAEP 2024 Results](https://www.nagb.gov/naep-results/reading.html), [NAEP Long-Term Trend](https://nationsreportcard.gov/highlights/ltt/2022/).

### Mathematics

| Grade | Year | At/Above Proficient | Below Basic | Source |
|-------|------|-------------------|-------------|--------|
| 4 | 2024 | 39% | 24% | NAEP 2024 |
| 8 | 2024 | ~27% | ~37% | NCES / NAEP 2024 |
| 12 | 2024 | Lower than 2019 | Higher than 2019 | NAGB, Sep 2025 |

Grade 4 math showed a modest 2-point recovery from the historic 2022 collapse — the only bright spot. Grade 8 math showed **no significant recovery**: ~73% of 8th graders are not proficient. The 2022 results represented the **largest score decline in NAEP math history** at grade 8 (an 8-point drop from 2019).

Internationally, OECD PISA 2022 confirmed an **"unprecedented decline"** in mathematics and reading skills among 15-year-olds. The U.S. remained behind Singapore, Hong Kong, Taiwan, South Korea, and Japan. (Reuters, Dec 2023; OECD PISA 2022)

### The Decline Predates COVID

NAEP data shows a decade of decline that **began before the pandemic:**

- Reading at all grades was trending down from mid-2010s peaks before COVID
- Math at grade 8 was flat from 2009–2019, then collapsed in 2022
- The pandemic **deepened** existing trends but did not create them
- The OECD called it "a continuation of a long-term decline since the early 2010s" — COVID was "only one contributing factor"

## 2. College Readiness

### ACT Results (Class of 2024)

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Average composite score | **19.4** (multi-year decline; pre-pandemic ~21) |
| Met all 4 benchmarks | **20%** |
| Met zero benchmarks | **43%** |
| Math readiness | **29%** |
| Reading readiness | 40% |
| English readiness | 51% |

Source: [ACT Inc., National Profile Report, Oct 2024](https://www.act.org).

Only **1 in 5** ACT-takers met all four college readiness benchmarks. **Nearly half met none.** Math readiness (29%) is the weakest area — consistent with NAEP trends.

### Remediation Reality

- **~40% of entering college students** require at least one developmental (remedial) course (Complete College America, 2024; NCES)
- At open-access institutions, math remediation rates exceed **60%**
- Only ~25% of students who start in developmental math earn a degree within 6 years, vs. ~50% for those starting in college-level courses
- COVID-era learning loss is projected to **increase remediation demand** through at least 2028

## 3. The "Digital Native" Myth

The label "digital native" obscures a critical distinction: **fluent consumer ≠ capable producer.**

- The 2018 International Computer and Information Literacy Study (ICILS), testing 42,000 eighth-graders across 14 countries, found **only 2%** were sufficiently proficient to justify the label, and **only 19%** could work independently with computers to gather and manage information (ICILS 2018; Strauss, Washington Post, Nov 2019)
- By the early 2020s, many Gen Z workers entered the workplace **without basic ICT skills** including touch typing (The Guardian, Feb 2023; WSJ, Aug 2024)
- Oxford English Dictionary chose **"brain rot"** as its 2024 word of the year, recognizing internet culture's impact on this generation

**Design implication:** IS 117 must teach filesystem navigation, terminal use, and Git from zero. The "digital native" assumption is empirically false for production-relevant skills.

## 4. Mental Health and Resilience

The mental health crisis is well-documented and severe:

| Metric | Value | Source |
|--------|-------|--------|
| Gen Z diagnosed with a mental health condition | **46%** | Harmony Healthcare IT, Jun 2025 |
| High schoolers reporting regular mental health struggles (COVID era) | **37%** | CDC, cited by Pew Research, Apr 2022 |
| 13-year-olds reading for fun daily | **14%** (record low) | NAEP LTT, 2023 |

Gen Z **proved less resilient than older cohorts** during COVID disruptions (Harari et al., *Current Psychology*, Aug 2022). They crave security in what they perceive as a "volatile, precarious, complex, and ambiguous" world (Wright, *Psychology Today*, Jan 2025). Many navigate economic stagnation and housing crises that contribute to demoralization (Burn-Murdoch, *Financial Times*, Jul 2025).

At the same time, Gen Z values **ambition, creativity, and curiosity** more than the general population (NielsenIQ, Aug 2023). The most important career factors: **skill development** (24%) and **income** (23%) (Varkey Foundation, Jan 2017).

**Design implication:** The Human Edge capability of Resilience Thinking (IS 219) directly addresses this. The Failure Mode Analysis and Incident Drill normalize failure as data. Studio cohort structure provides built-in community. Mental health accommodations must be routine, not exceptional.

## 5. Screen Time and Attention

- **41%** of Gen Z spend 3+ hours/day on computers for non-school purposes (Sparks and Honey, 2014)
- **77%** agree: "When nothing occupies my attention, the first thing I do is reach for my phone" (Microsoft, 2015)
- OECD PISA found reading scores **declined more** among students spending more time on devices
- A 2019 meta-analysis across ~20 countries found excessive screen time **negatively correlated with grades, language fluency, and math ability** — effects stronger among adolescents (Adelantado-Renau et al., *JAMA Pediatrics*, Sep 2019)

**Design implication:** The studio model's project-based structure with shipped artifacts counteracts passive consumption. Weekly deliverables create structure for students whose default mode is scroll, not build.

## 6. The Opportunity: AI Helps Novices Most

Here is the hopeful data point: **AI productivity gains are largest for the least-experienced workers.**

| Study | Finding |
|-------|---------|
| MIT RCT | Professionals using ChatGPT completed tasks **40% faster with 18% higher quality**; **largest gains for lower-baseline performers** |
| Stanford / Fortune 500 | AI-assisted customer support showed **+14% average productivity; +30–35% for least-experienced workers** |

Source: [MIT](https://news.mit.edu), [Stanford GSB](https://www.gsb.stanford.edu).

AI effectively **disseminates expert best practices to novices**. A student from an under-resourced background who learns to use AI tools effectively can leapfrog to expert-level productivity. But only if they also learn to **evaluate** AI output — because AI that is wrong at expert speed does more damage than no AI at all.

This is the program's thesis in one finding: **give students the human skills to judge AI, and the AI amplifies them beyond what either could achieve alone.**

## 8. Design Implications

The data demands specific design responses — all present in the BS in Enterprise AI:

### Start from Zero
IS 117 → IS 118 must assume **no prior terminal, Git, or programming experience** and **below-proficient reading and math skills**. This is not pessimism — it's what NAEP requires.

### Build Reading Through Doing
Long reading assignments will fail. Short, purposeful readings immediately applied to builds work. The "inquiry" model — read 3 pages, produce 1 page — respects measured attention and reading levels while building the skill.

### Make Failure Safe Before Making It Required
Resilience Thinking (IS 219) comes in Year 3, but failure tolerance must be scaffolded from Week 1. Given mental health data, early courses need clear rubrics, multiple revision opportunities, and "failure as data" framing.

### Ship Artifacts, Not Abstractions
A generation shaped by passive consumption needs to **produce**. Shipped artifacts every semester give concrete proof of capability, counteracting the learned helplessness many carry from K-12.

### Community by Design
Gen Z reports increasing social isolation despite constant connectivity. The studio cohort (same ~20 peers across 8 courses) provides **built-in community** — the thing they need but won't create on their own.

### Financial Motivation Is Real
The salary data ($110K–$150K entry-level) is a legitimate enrollment driver for students who watched their parents struggle through inflation. Present career outcomes honestly and prominently — this cohort responds to pragmatism.

### AI Fluency ≠ AI Judgment
Students may arrive with ChatGPT familiarity but without the ability to evaluate AI output. The AI Audit Log (IS 218) addresses this: you cannot judge AI output without the foundational knowledge to recognize when it's wrong.

## Gen Alpha Preview (Arriving ~2029–2030)

Students born ~2012+ will enter by 2030. Early indicators:

- NAEP 2024 data for 4th graders (born ~2014–2015): **40% below Basic in reading** — the worst cohort entering the pipeline
- K-12 experience more disrupted than any prior generation (pandemic closures during elementary years)
- Growing up with generative AI as a **baseline assumption**, not a novelty
- Mobile phone bans spreading in schools globally (2024+) — institutional recognition that device management is failing

Gen Alpha freshmen may arrive with even weaker foundational skills but more intuitive AI-tool fluency. The program's design — leveraging AI tools while developing the human capabilities that make AI use valuable — is positioned for this transition.
