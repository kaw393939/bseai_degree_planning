# Executive Summary

**BS in Enterprise AI — The Human Edge**  
New Jersey Institute of Technology · Ying Wu College of Computing

---

## The Opportunity

AI is automating cognitive work at an accelerating rate. The METR benchmark shows AI task-completion capability doubling approximately every 3 months — and accelerating (METR, February 2026). Employers are already hiring "Forward-Deployed AI Engineers," "AI Deployment Engineers," and "AI Product Engineers" at $137,000–$350,000 (Morningstar, OpenAI, Titan, WorkWhile — see [Job Market Evidence](/bseai_degree_planning/evidence/job-market/)).

These roles don't require someone who can write code. They require someone who can **direct AI agents, evaluate their output, and take responsibility for the result** — while collaborating across product, design, and research functions.

No existing degree program at NJIT — or nationally — is designed from first principles to produce this professional.

### The Students Who Need This Most

The students entering this program reflect a national reality that most curricula ignore: ~65% of 12th-graders read below NAEP Proficient (NAGB, Sep 2025), only 29% of ACT-takers are math college-ready, and only 2% of 8th-graders demonstrated digital production proficiency (ICILS 2018). Add 46% Gen Z mental health diagnosis rates, and the picture is clear: these students need a program that starts from zero and builds capability through shipped artifacts, not one that assumes preparation it cannot count on. The studio model is the evidence-based response. (See [Who Walks in the Door](/bseai_degree_planning/evidence/student-profile/).)

### The Fiscal Context

This is not only an educational response — it is an economic one. The U.S. faces $38.5T in gross federal debt (~100% of GDP) with ~$1T/yr in interest alone (CBO, Feb 2026). There are three exits: tax more, inflate, or grow productivity. The first two are regressive. The third requires people who can deploy AI effectively — the bottleneck is human capital, not compute. Goldman Sachs projects AI could add +1.4 pp/yr to productivity growth; McKinsey projects +0.5–3.4 pp/yr with automation. But every major study conditions those gains on skilled adoption. Big 4 hyperscalers are deploying ~$600B+ in AI infrastructure in 2026 alone. The government has responded with a White House AI Education Task Force and 90+ action items (Apr 2025). This program produces exactly the professionals these investments require. (See [The Productivity Imperative](/bseai_degree_planning/evidence/productivity-imperative/).)

## The Degree

**8 studio courses** over 4 years, each pairing a technical domain with an irreplaceable **Human Edge** capability:

| Year | Courses | Human Capabilities | Technical Foundation |
|------|---------|-------------------|---------------------|
| 1 | IS 117 → IS 118 | Disciplined Inquiry + Professional Judgment | Web development, JavaScript, full-stack apps, testing, CI/CD |
| 2 | IS 218 → IS 265 | Resilience Thinking + Problem Finding | Linux, containers, infrastructure, enterprise stakeholder analysis |
| 3 | IS 331 → IS 390 | Epistemic Humility + Systems Thinking | Databases, knowledge graphs, systems design, AI architecture |
| 4 | IS 425 → IS 482 | Accountable Leadership + Translation | Production AI systems, client delivery, community AI training |

**Supported by:** CS 100, CS 114, MATH 111/211/226, DS 100, IS 247, IS 350, IS 392, IS 465, IS 480, and additional applied courses (IS 385, IS 491, IS 485).

---

## What Makes This Different

1. **Human capabilities are graded, not optional.** Each studio produces a Human Edge artifact (inquiry log, AI audit log, failure mode analysis, etc.) with real grade weight.

2. **40/60 hard-way/agentic split.** 40% of work builds foundational understanding. 60% develops AI-directed workflow — the actual skill employers pay for.

3. **Portfolio, not transcript.** Graduates present 8 shipped projects with increasing sophistication, plus Demo Day presentations starting Semester 2 (IS 118 onward).

4. **AI-native but AI-skeptical.** Students use AI tools from Day 1 but are trained to verify, evaluate, and override. The AI Audit Log (IS 118) documents what students accepted, rejected, and modified from AI — with reasoning.

5. **Studio, not lecture.** 20–25 student cohorts. Every course produces deployable artifacts. Real feedback cycles.

## Accreditation Alignment

The degree is designed to meet:

- **ABET CAC Information Systems criteria:** ≥30 SCH in IS topics (app dev, programming, data/DB, IT infrastructure, systems analysis, project management), ≥15 SCH in an IS environment, major project (IS 425)
- **MSCHE Standards III, V, VI:** Rigorous curriculum design, documented assessment of student learning, adequate resources and institutional support
- **ABET Student Outcomes:** All five general outcomes (analyze, design, communicate, ethical judgment, teamwork) are explicitly mapped to Human Edge capabilities and assessed through portfolio artifacts
