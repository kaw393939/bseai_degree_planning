# ABET CAC Alignment

This document maps the BS in Enterprise AI to the ABET Computing Accreditation Commission (CAC) criteria for **Information Systems** programs (2025–2026 cycle). The degree is designed to meet or exceed all general and program-specific criteria.

---

## General Criteria: Student Outcomes

ABET CAC requires that by graduation, students can demonstrate five outcomes. The table below maps each to Human Edge capabilities and assessment evidence.

| ABET Student Outcome | Human Edge Mapping | Where Assessed | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **(1) Analyze complex computing problems** and apply principles of computing and relevant disciplines | Disciplined Inquiry (IS 117), Problem Finding (IS 265), Systems Thinking (IS 390) | IS 117 inquiry logs, IS 265 problem framing memos, IS 390 requirements documents | Students decompose ambiguous problems using issue trees, stakeholder analysis, and systems decomposition — not just technical analysis |
| **(2) Design, implement, and evaluate** a computing-based solution to meet requirements | Professional Judgment (IS 218), Resilience Thinking (IS 219), Epistemic Humility (IS 331) | IS 218 deployed apps + AI audit logs, IS 219 containerized services + failure mode analyses, IS 331 data pipelines + validation | Every studio produces a deployed, tested artifact. Evaluation is explicit: students document what works, what fails, and why |
| **(3) Communicate effectively** in a variety of professional contexts | Translation (IS 118), Problem Finding (IS 265), Accountable Leadership (IS 425) | IS 118 translation briefs, IS 265 persuasion exercises, IS 425 Demo Day presentations | Translation is a core Human Edge — not "write well" but "make the invisible visible for this audience" |
| **(4) Recognize professional responsibilities** and make informed judgments considering ethical, legal, and social impacts | Professional Judgment (IS 218), Epistemic Humility (IS 331), Accountable Leadership (IS 425) + IS 350 (Ethics, dedicated course) | IS 218 AI audit logs, IS 350 ethics case studies, IS 425 postmortems and leadership reflections | Ethics is addressed both in a dedicated course (IS 350) and woven through the spine via Human Edge artifacts |
| **(5) Function effectively** as a member or leader of a team | All studios (cohort model, 20–25 students) | Peer evaluations, commit evidence, team contracts, Demo Day team presentations | Studio model requires collaboration by design. IS 425 capstone requires team delivery to a real client |

## General Criteria: Curriculum

| ABET Requirement | How EAI Meets It |
|---|---|
| **Appropriate math/statistics/science** | MATH 111 (Calculus I), MATH 211 or DS 110 (Linear Algebra), MATH 226 (Discrete Analysis), DS 100 (Probability & Statistics) = 12+ SCH math/stat |
| **≥30 SCH of current computing topics** | 8 spine courses × 3 SCH = 24 SCH + IS 350, IS 247, IS 392, IS 465, IS 480 = 39+ SCH computing |
| **Computing topics cover tools/practice** | Every studio: terminal, Git, CI/CD, deployment, testing, AI tools (from IS 117 onward) |
| **Security and privacy** | IS 219 (auth, secrets management, security), IS 350 (ethics including privacy), IS 331 (data governance) |
| **Societal impacts** | IS 350 (Computers, Society & Ethics — dedicated course), IS 265 (organizational impact analysis), IS 425 (responsible AI deployment) |

## Program-Specific Criteria: Information Systems

ABET's IS-specific criteria require coverage in the following areas. Each is mapped to specific courses.

| IS Criterion | Courses | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Application development** | IS 117, IS 118, IS 218, IS 219 | Students build progressively complex applications: static sites → interactive apps → full-stack → containerized services |
| **Programming** | CS 100 (Python), IS 117–118 (HTML/CSS/JS), IS 218 (full-stack), IS 219 (systems scripting), IS 331 (SQL/queries) | Python foundations + web stack + systems programming + data querying |
| **Data and information management (databases)** | IS 331 (relational, graph, vector, pipelines, data quality), IS 425 (knowledge graphs + vector retrieval in production) | IS 331 is a full database course. IS 425 integrates graph + vector in a capstone system |
| **IT infrastructure** | IS 219 (Linux, containers, networking, orchestration, observability), IS 425 (production deployment) | IS 219 is a dedicated infrastructure course covering the full stack from terminal to cloud |
| **Systems analysis and design** | IS 390 (requirements analysis, architecture, tradeoffs, evaluation plans), IS 265 (business process modeling, stakeholder analysis) | IS 390 is the systems analysis course. IS 265 provides organizational context |
| **Systems acquisition** | IS 390 (build vs. buy analysis, vendor evaluation), IS 425 (integration of third-party AI services) | Covered in systems design and capstone |
| **Project management** | IS 265 (workflow prototyping, project scoping), IS 390 (SDLC planning), IS 425 (client project management) | Project management is practiced, not just taught — students manage real deliverables |
| **Role of IS in organizations** | IS 265 (business process modeling, stakeholder analysis, organizational dynamics) | IS 265 is specifically about understanding IS in organizational context |
| **≥15 SCH in an IS environment** | IS 350 (Ethics), IS 247 (UX), IS 392 (Applied AI), IS 465 (ML), IS 480 (AI QA), IS 491 (Practicum) | Applied AI, ML, UX, Ethics, QA, and industry practicum provide broad IS context |
| **Major project (IS 425 capstone)** | IS 425: production AI system with knowledge graph + HITL + evaluation + Demo Day | Real client, real problem, production deployment, industry judging |
| **Appropriate math/stat modeling** | MATH 111, MATH 211/DS 110, MATH 226, DS 100, IS 465 (applied ML) | Calculus, linear algebra, discrete math, probability/statistics, applied ML |

## Continuous Improvement Plan

ABET requires documented continuous improvement. The EAI program implements:

| Mechanism | Frequency | What It Measures |
|---|---|---|
| **Portfolio review** | Every semester | Quality and progression of Human Edge artifacts across cohort |
| **Demo Day rubrics** | Starting Year 2 | External judges + faculty score technical depth, communication, evidence, responsibility |
| **AI Audit Log analysis** | Every semester (IS 218+) | Are students developing informed judgment about AI output? |
| **Industry Advisory Board** | Annual | Are program outcomes aligned with employer needs? |
| **Graduate surveys** | 1-year and 3-year post-graduation | Are PEOs being achieved? |
| **Job placement data** | Annual | Employment rates, roles, salaries |
