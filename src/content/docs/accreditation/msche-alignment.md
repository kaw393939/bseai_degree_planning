# MSCHE Standards Alignment

NJIT is accredited by the Middle States Commission on Higher Education (MSCHE). This document maps the BS in Enterprise AI to MSCHE's Standards for Accreditation (Fourteenth Edition, effective July 1, 2023), with particular attention to Standards III, V, and VI — the standards most relevant to new program approval.

---

## Substantive Change Classification

**Key question:** Does this program require MSCHE prior approval?

Based on MSCHE guidance:

- ✅ NJIT already offers bachelor's degrees — no new credential level
- ✅ The program is housed in the Ying Wu College of Computing, which already offers IS degrees — this is a logical extension of existing offerings
- ✅ Delivery is in-person/hybrid — no new delivery method
- ✅ No additional locations or third-party providers

**Likely classification:** Not a significant departure. Standard institutional governance approvals (department → college → provost → board) should suffice, with notification to MSCHE via the Institution Portal.

However, the program director should confirm with NJIT's MSCHE liaison whether the "Enterprise AI" designation triggers a significant departure review. If so, the documentation below supports the required submission.

## Standard III: Design and Delivery of the Student Learning Experience

> *"An institution provides students with learning experiences that are characterized by rigor and coherence at all program, certificate, and institutional levels and are consistent with higher education expectations."*

### How EAI Meets Standard III

| Element | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Rigor** | Studio model with 20–25 student cohorts, deployed artifacts every semester, Demo Day presentations scored by industry judges and faculty. No inflated grades — every deliverable must work. |
| **Coherence** | 8-course spine with explicit sequencing rationale (see [Course Sequence](/bseai_degree_planning/curriculum/course-sequence/)). Each studio builds on cumulative Human Edge capabilities. Curriculum map shows Introduced → Reinforced → Mastered progression. |
| **Learning outcomes** | Program Educational Objectives (3–5 year) + Student Outcomes (at graduation) + Course-level learning objectives (Bloom's-aligned) for every course. All documented in [Program Outcomes](/bseai_degree_planning/program/student-outcomes/). |
| **General education** | Students complete NJIT's standard general education requirements. The EAI spine supplements (not replaces) GE coursework. |
| **Faculty qualifications** | 4 committed faculty with industry + academic credentials. Studio courses require AI fluency — all faculty actively use AI tools in research and practice. |
| **Academic integrity** | The 40/60 split makes AI use explicit, documented, and assessed — not hidden. AI Audit Logs (IS 218+) require students to document what they accepted, rejected, and modified from AI with reasoning. |
| **Accessibility** | Studio model provides intensive faculty-student interaction. Remediation-as-a-feature philosophy: Year 1 starts from zero and builds digital literacy from scratch. |
| **Current technology** | AI tools (Claude, GPT, Cursor, Claude Code) integrated from Day 1. Infrastructure courses (IS 219) cover containers, cloud, and modern deployment. Content refreshed every semester per the 40/60 split model. |

## Standard V: Educational Effectiveness Assessment

> *"Assessment of student learning and achievement demonstrates that the institution's students have accomplished educational goals consistent with their program of study."*

### Assessment Architecture

| Level | What | How | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Course** | Learning objectives (Bloom's-aligned) | Portfolio artifacts, deployed projects, Human Edge artifacts, studio checkoffs | Every semester |
| **Program** | Student Outcomes (ABET-aligned, 5 outcomes) | Cumulative portfolio review, capstone evaluation (IS 425), Demo Day rubrics | Annual cohort review |
| **Post-graduation** | Program Educational Objectives | Graduate surveys (1-year, 3-year), employer surveys, job placement data | Ongoing |

### Key Assessment Mechanisms

- **Human Edge Artifacts:** Each studio produces a graded artifact specifically assessing the course's human capability (inquiry log, translation brief, AI audit log, failure mode analysis, etc.)
- **Portfolio Accumulation:** Students build 8 projects over 4 years, reviewed for quality and progression
- **Demo Day:** Starting Semester 2 (IS 118 onward), external judges score presentations on technical depth, communication, evidence, and responsibility
- **AI Audit Logs:** From IS 218 onward, students document their judgment about AI output — creating a longitudinal record of developing professional judgment
- **Capstone (IS 425):** Production AI system built for a real client, with postmortem and improvement plan

### Continuous Improvement Loop

```
Assess → Analyze → Improve → Re-assess
```

- Faculty review portfolio artifacts and Demo Day results each semester
- Industry Advisory Board provides annual feedback on outcome relevance
- Curriculum adjustments made for next cohort based on evidence
- Assessment results documented and available for review

## Standard VI: Planning, Resources, and Institutional Improvement

> *"The institution's planning processes, resources, and structures are aligned with each other and are sufficient to fulfill its mission and goals."*

### Resource Alignment

| Resource | Current Status |
|---|---|
| **Faculty** | 4 committed instructors. Studio cap of 20–25 enables intensive mentoring. Faculty actively use AI tools in practice. |
| **Facilities** | Standard NJIT computing labs. Students use personal machines + cloud infrastructure. No specialized hardware required. |
| **Library/IT** | NJIT library provides standard computing holdings. AI tools are cloud-based ($20/month student cost or institutional license). |
| **Budget** | Minimal incremental cost: 7 of 8 spine courses use existing catalog numbers. Primary investment is faculty time for studio preparation. |
| **Student support** | Standard NJIT advising + studio model provides intensive cohort-based support. IS 117 starts from zero — remediation is built in. |

### Planning

- **Phase 1 (Fall 2026):** IS 117, IS 218, IS 265, IS 331, IS 390 ready as studios
- **Phase 2 (Spring 2027):** IS 118 (proposed new course), IS 219 as studio
- **Phase 3 (Fall 2027):** Full spine operational (IS 117 through IS 425)
- **Industry partnerships:** Center for Enterprise AI provides project pipeline, industry mentors, and Demo Day judges

### Institutional Fit

The BS in Enterprise AI sits within the Ying Wu College of Computing's Information Systems department. It:

- Uses existing IS course numbers where possible (minimizing catalog impact)
- Leverages existing math/CS prerequisite courses
- Complements (not competes with) existing CS and IT programs by focusing on the *human orchestration layer* rather than traditional development or infrastructure
- Addresses documented employer demand for AI-fluent professionals who combine technical skill with judgment and communication
