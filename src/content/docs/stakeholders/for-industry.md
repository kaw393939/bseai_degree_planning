# For Industry Partners

The BS in Enterprise AI at NJIT produces graduates who can **build, evaluate, and take accountability for production AI systems**. They don't just know AI theory — they've shipped working systems, debugged production failures, and presented to critical audiences.

Here's how to partner with the program.

---

## What Our Graduates Can Do

By graduation, every BS in EAI student has:

- **Built 8 deployed projects** across a four-year studio spine
- **Managed a real client relationship** with deliverables, feedback, and accountability (IS 425 capstone)
- **Designed and built a production AI system** with knowledge graphs, human-in-the-loop workflows, evaluation harnesses, and observability
- **Maintained an AI Audit Log** documenting every decision about when to trust and when to override AI output
- **Survived an incident drill** — their professor broke their production system and they had to detect, diagnose, and recover under time pressure
- **Presented at Demo Day** — live demonstration to industry judges with Q&A about every design and engineering decision

These graduates arrive on day one with **professional habits**, not just academic credentials.

---

## Engagement Opportunities

### Capstone Projects (IS 425 — Applied AI Systems)

The most impactful way to engage. Your organization provides:
- A real problem with real data (appropriately scoped for one semester)
- A point of contact for student teams (1–2 hours/month)
- Participation in Demo Day judging (end of semester)

In return you get:
- A working prototype or proof-of-concept (knowledge graph + HITL + evaluation harness)
- Exposure to talent before they hit the job market
- Fresh perspectives on your AI challenges
- No cost to you (students work as part of their course)

**Ideal capstone projects:** Enterprise search, document processing, knowledge management, compliance workflows, customer support augmentation — any problem where AI needs structured knowledge and human oversight.

### Demo Day Judging

Join our end-of-semester Demo Day as an industry judge:
- See 5–10 student teams present working AI systems
- Evaluate engineering quality, communication, and honest self-assessment
- Network with graduating students
- Time commitment: 3–4 hours per semester

### Advisory Board

Join the BS in EAI Advisory Board:
- Review curriculum annually
- Provide industry perspective on skills and tools
- Connect program to hiring pipelines
- Time commitment: 2 meetings/year + optional events

### Internship Pipeline

Our students are ready for AI-related internships after Year 2 (they've shipped a full-stack application with testing and CI/CD). After Year 3, they've added infrastructure, data systems, and knowledge engineering.

| After Year | Skills Available | Ideal Internship Roles |
|-----------|-----------------|----------------------|
| Year 2 | Full-stack dev, testing, CI/CD, AI-assisted development | Software engineering intern |
| Year 3 | + Infrastructure, containers, observability, data systems | AI systems intern, data engineering intern |
| Year 4 | + Knowledge engineering, HITL design, evaluation, client delivery | AI engineering intern, solutions architecture intern |

### Guest Lectures and Mentorship

Share real-world experience:
- Guest lecture on production AI challenges (1 hour)
- Mentor a student team during capstone (1 hour/week for one semester)
- Participate in mock stakeholder panels for IS 265 (2 hours)
- Serve as a "skeptical stakeholder" for student persuasion presentations

## What Makes These Graduates Different

| Traditional CS/IS Graduate | BS in EAI Graduate |
|---------------------------|-------------------|
| Can write code | Can write code AND evaluate AI-generated code |
| Learned theory in lectures | Built 8 deployed projects in studios |
| Academic projects | Real client engagement (IS 425) |
| No systematic AI evaluation skills | AI Audit Log discipline from IS 218 |
| No knowledge engineering | Knowledge graphs + vector retrieval + hybrid systems |
| First production experience at first job | Production experience from Year 2 |
| No incident response training | Incident drills, postmortems, failure mode analysis |
| Can present final projects to professors | Demo Day with industry judges |

### The Human Edge

Beyond technical skills, our graduates have been systematically trained in capabilities that can't be automated:

- **Judgment** — they know when to trust AI and when to override it
- **Communication** — they can explain technical decisions to non-technical stakeholders
- **Accountability** — they take ownership of failures and learn from them
- **Problem Finding** — they can identify the real problem, not just the stated one
- **Resilience** — they've practiced failure response under pressure
