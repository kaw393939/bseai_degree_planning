# For Administrators

**The 60-second version:** The BS in Enterprise AI is a strategic reconfiguration of NJIT's existing IS program to produce graduates who can build, evaluate, and take accountability for production AI systems — the fastest-growing segment of the tech workforce. It uses mostly existing resources, aligns with ABET and MSCHE requirements, and positions NJIT as a national leader in AI education.

---

## What It Is

An eight-course studio spine that takes students from zero technical knowledge to building production-grade AI systems with knowledge graphs, human-in-the-loop workflows, and evaluation harnesses — all while developing the permanent human capabilities (judgment, inquiry, resilience, accountability) that differentiate humans from AI.

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Degree | BS in Enterprise AI (within Informatics) |
| Studio spine | 8 courses, sequenced, cohort-based |
| New courses needed | 1 (IS 118); IS 425 already exists, needs minor update |
| Existing catalog numbers used | IS 117, IS 218, IS 219, IS 265, IS 331, IS 390 |
| Faculty required | 4 (identified and willing) |
| Target launch | Fall 2026 |
| Accreditation pathway | ABET CAC (IS criteria) + MSCHE |

## What Makes It Different

### The Human Edge Framework

Every course develops one of eight Human Edge capabilities — permanent skills AI cannot replicate:

| Capability | Course | What It Means |
|-----------|--------|--------------|
| Disciplined Inquiry | IS 117 | Asking the right questions |
| Translation | IS 118 | Making the invisible visible |
| Professional Judgment | IS 218 | Knowing when to override the machine |
| Problem Finding | IS 265 | Identifying the real problem |
| Resilience Thinking | IS 219 | Designing for failure |
| Epistemic Humility | IS 331 | Knowing what you don't know |
| Systems Thinking | IS 390 | Seeing the whole system |
| Accountable Leadership | IS 425 | Standing behind your work |

### The 40/60 Split

Every course: 40% manual work (building understanding) + 60% AI-directed work (building velocity). Students learn to use AI as professionals — critically, selectively, and with accountability.

### The Capstone

IS 425: Real clients, real problems, production-grade AI systems, Demo Day with industry judges. Students don't just learn about AI — they ship AI and defend it.

## Accreditation Alignment

### ABET CAC (Computing Accreditation Commission)
- All 5 ABET IS student outcomes mapped and assessed through I/R/M progression
- IS-specific criteria met: ≥30 SCH in IS topics (program provides 39 SCH)
- Continuous improvement mechanisms built into assessment architecture

### MSCHE (Middle States Commission on Higher Education)
- Likely classification: **not a significant departure** from existing IS program
- Standards III (Design and Delivery), V (Educational Effectiveness), VI (Planning, Resources, Improvement) all addressed
- Substantive change documentation prepared
