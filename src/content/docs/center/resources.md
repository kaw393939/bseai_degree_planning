### The Human Edge Framework

The eight Human Edge capabilities — Disciplined Inquiry, Professional Judgment, Resilience Thinking, Problem Finding, Epistemic Humility, Systems Thinking, Accountable Leadership, and Translation — are published as a reusable curriculum design tool.

Other programs can adopt the framework without adopting our specific courses. The capabilities are technology-agnostic and will remain relevant regardless of which AI tools exist in five years.

- [The Human Edge Framework](/bseai_degree_planning/curriculum/outcomes/) — full capability definitions and progression model
- [Curriculum Map](/bseai_degree_planning/program/curriculum-map/) — how capabilities map to courses and assessments

### The 40/60 Policy

The [40/60 policy](/bseai_degree_planning/program/forty-sixty-policy/) — 40% manual skill building, 60% AI-directed development — is our answer to the question every technical educator is asking: "How do I teach when AI can do the homework?"

We publish the policy with implementation guidance so other programs can adapt it. The ratio is not sacred; the principle is: students must understand the fundamentals before they direct AI to scale them.

### The Context Pack

The [Context Pack](/bseai_degree_planning/program/context-pack/) is our structured framework for AI-assisted work. It teaches students to prepare the context an AI assistant needs before asking it to produce output. Published with templates and examples at each proficiency level.

## Educator Resources

### For Faculty Integrating AI Into Existing Courses

Not every institution will launch a new degree. Many faculty are trying to integrate AI responsibly into courses that already exist. The Center publishes resources for this common challenge:

- **The 40/60 framework** — a principled approach to balancing manual skill-building with AI-assisted productivity
- **The Context Pack model** — teaching students to prepare AI prompts as a professional skill, not a shortcut
- **Assessment strategies** — how to assess student learning when AI can produce correct output (hint: assess the judgment, not the output)
- **AI Audit Log** — a portable assessment artifact usable in any course where students use AI tools

### For Programs Designing AI Curricula

The [program design](/bseai_degree_planning/program/program-objectives/) section documents our end-to-end curriculum architecture:

- Program Educational Objectives (PEOs)
- Student Outcomes aligned to ABET CAC criteria
- Bloom's-taxonomy learning objectives for every course
- Curriculum mapping (objectives to courses to assessments)
- The studio model (cohort-based, project-every-semester, portfolio on graduation)
