# Community Engagement

The Center for Enterprise AI makes NJIT a neighbor, not just an institution on a hill. Every year, the program's seniors give back to the city that hosts them — and in doing so, prove the ultimate mastery of the Human Edge.

---

## The Annual Community AI Training Event

Each spring, seniors in IS 482 (Service Learning: Community AI Training) design, organize, and deliver a **free public AI literacy training event** in Newark. The Center provides the infrastructure; students provide the content and facilitation.

### Who Attends

The event is designed for people who are affected by AI every day but have never had anyone explain it to them honestly:

- **Small business owners** trying to figure out if AI tools are worth the investment
- **Nonprofit staff** wondering how to use AI responsibly with limited budgets
- **Public school teachers** whose students are already using AI and who need to understand what it does
- **Municipal employees** navigating new AI-enabled government services
- **Newark residents** who want to understand what AI means for their jobs, their privacy, and their city

### What Happens

The event includes hands-on workshops (not lectures), covering topics such as:

- What AI actually is — and is not — in plain language
- How to evaluate AI tools for your work (and when to say no)
- Privacy, security, and what happens to your data
- Practical exercises with real AI tools in a safe, guided environment
- Q&A sessions where honest answers are the rule, not marketing language

### Institutional Continuity

The Center maintains the relationships and infrastructure so each cohort starts strong:

- **Community partner contacts** carried forward year over year
- **Venue partnerships** maintained by the Center (not by graduating students)
- **Materials archive** so each year's cohort can build on what worked before
- **Outreach lists and channels** maintained and grown annually
- **Feedback history** so recurring problems get fixed, not repeated

### Funding

The Dean of Ying Wu College of Computing has committed to fundraising for the community training component. Event costs (venue, food, materials, equipment) are supported through institutional funding and sponsorships coordinated by the Center.

## Newark Partnerships

The Center actively maintains relationships with Newark organizations:

- **Newark Public Schools** — teacher training and student engagement
- **Local business associations** — small business AI readiness workshops
- **Newark nonprofits** — responsible AI adoption for organizations with limited technical staff
- **City of Newark** — municipal AI literacy and responsible deployment
- **NJIT community programs** — integration with existing university outreach initiatives

These partnerships are not one-semester arrangements. The Center maintains them as long-term institutional commitments, ensuring that every year's students have real community partners to work with.

---

## Public Resources

Materials developed for community training are published openly:

- **Workshop curricula** — lesson plans, facilitator guides, participant handouts
- **AI literacy reference cards** — plain-language explainers for common AI concepts
- **Evaluation frameworks** — how to assess whether an AI tool is right for your organization
- **Community training playbook** — how other universities can replicate this model

Everything is open-licensed. If other universities use NJIT's community training model, that is a success, not a threat.

---

## Why This Matters

AI literacy is not a luxury. The people most affected by AI — workers whose tasks are being automated, small business owners evaluating expensive tools, teachers whose students are submitting AI-generated work, residents interacting with AI-powered government services — are often the last to receive honest, accessible education about what AI can and cannot do.

The Center for Enterprise AI exists to close that gap. NJIT's seniors do not just learn about AI — they teach it to the people who need it most. That is the Human Edge in action.
