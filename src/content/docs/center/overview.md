# Center for Enterprise AI

**Department of Informatics** | Ying Wu College of Computing | NJIT
**Director:** Keith Williams, Professor of Informatics

---

The Center for Enterprise AI (CEAI) is the institutional home for NJIT's BS in Enterprise AI program. It provides the operational infrastructure, community partnerships, and knowledge-sharing platform that turn a degree program into an institution with national impact.

## Mission

Train the next generation of enterprise AI leaders while giving back to the community that hosts us. Publish what we learn so other educators can adapt it.

---

## What the Center Does

### 1. Operations Hub

The Center manages the signature events and project pipelines that make the studio spine work:

**Demo Day (IS 425, Senior Fall)**
The Center coordinates NJIT's annual technical showcase — venue, industry evaluator invitations, A/V, streaming, and recording. Demo Day is where graduating seniors present their applied AI systems to faculty and industry professionals. The Center ensures this event is reliable and scalable, not dependent on any single faculty member.

**Community AI Training (IS 482, Senior Spring)**
The Center manages the annual Newark community AI training event — the university's most visible contribution to its home city. This includes maintaining community partnerships, venue relationships, outreach infrastructure, and the training materials archive. Each year's cohort inherits what the previous cohort built, so quality compounds.

**Project Pipeline (IS 265 + IS 425)**
The Center solicits and curates real enterprise problems from local businesses, nonprofits, and NJIT internal operations. IS 265 students (midpoint capstone, sophomore spring) work on scoped problem analysis and prototyping. IS 425 students (senior capstone) build production-grade solutions. The Center maintains the relationship with each external partner across semesters, providing continuity that individual courses cannot.

### 2. Open Knowledge Platform

The Center publishes educational resources and research openly, establishing NJIT as a thought leader in AI education:

- **Course materials** — syllabi, Context Pack templates, assessment rubrics, the 40/60 framework documentation. Open-licensed for adaptation.
- **The Human Edge framework** — the 8-capability model as a reusable curriculum design tool for any institution.
- **Research and analysis** — benchmark tracking, labor market analysis, and evidence-based program design rationale.
- **Educator transition guides** — practical resources for faculty at NJIT and other universities integrating AI into existing courses.

If twenty universities adapt the Human Edge framework, NJIT is the origin point. That is brand equity that money cannot buy.

### 3. Industry Bridge

The Center is the single point of contact for employers who want to engage with the program:

- **Submit project problems** for capstone courses (IS 265 and IS 425)
- **Attend Demo Day** and recruit graduating seniors
- **Sponsor community training events** in Newark
- **Partner on applied research** in enterprise AI deployment
- **Provide internship placements** for students completing the midpoint capstone (IS 265)

### 4. Community Engagement

NJIT is an anchor institution in Newark — one of New Jersey's most important and historically underserved cities. The Center fulfills the university's community mission through:

- **Annual community AI training** organized and delivered by senior students
- **Partnerships with Newark organizations** — nonprofits, small businesses, public schools, municipal agencies
- **Public resources** — materials from community training events are published for anyone to use
- **Visible institutional commitment** — the Center demonstrates that NJIT does not just study AI; it brings AI literacy to its neighbors

## Why a Center?

A degree program trains students. A center builds an institution.

**For NJIT:** The Center elevates the BS in Enterprise AI from a degree to a strategic initiative. Centers attract grants, donations, and press in ways that degree programs alone cannot. It positions NJIT as a national thought leader in AI education.

**For accreditation:** ABET and MSCHE value institutional support structures. The Center demonstrates the university's commitment to sustaining the program and its community mission — not just launching it.

**For faculty:** A center with published output, public events, and industry partnerships is a draw for faculty recruitment. Candidates are not joining a department — they are joining a center with national visibility.

**For students:** The Center provides the infrastructure that makes signature experiences — Demo Day, community training, real-world projects — reliable and high-quality. Students benefit from institutional memory across cohorts.

**For Newark:** The Center is how NJIT's technical expertise reaches the community. AI literacy should not be a luxury available only to those who can afford a CS degree.
