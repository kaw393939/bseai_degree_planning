# Student Outcomes

Student Outcomes describe what graduates can demonstrate **at the point of graduation.** They combine ABET CAC general outcomes with the program's unique Human Edge capabilities.

---

## ABET-Aligned Outcomes

By the time of graduation, students will be able to:

### SO-1: Analyze Complex Computing Problems
**Analyze complex computing problems** and apply principles of computing, mathematics, and relevant disciplines to identify solutions — including problems involving AI components with probabilistic behavior.

*Human Edge connection:* Disciplined Inquiry (IS 117), Problem Finding (IS 265), Systems Thinking (IS 390)

### SO-2: Design, Implement, and Evaluate
**Design, implement, and evaluate** a computing-based solution to meet a given set of requirements in the context of the program's discipline — including solutions that integrate AI agents, knowledge graphs, and human-in-the-loop workflows.

*Human Edge connection:* Professional Judgment (IS 218), Resilience Thinking (IS 219), Accountable Leadership (IS 425)

### SO-3: Communicate Effectively
**Communicate effectively** in a variety of professional contexts — translating complex technical concepts for diverse audiences and presenting evidence-based recommendations to stakeholders.

*Human Edge connection:* Translation (IS 118), Problem Finding (IS 265), Accountable Leadership (IS 425)

### SO-4: Ethical and Professional Responsibility
**Recognize professional responsibilities** and make informed judgments in computing practice based on legal and ethical principles — including the specific responsibilities of deploying AI systems that affect people's lives.

*Human Edge connection:* Professional Judgment (IS 218), Epistemic Humility (IS 331), Accountable Leadership (IS 425), IS 350 (Ethics)

### SO-5: Team Effectiveness
**Function effectively** as a member or leader of a team engaged in activities appropriate to the program's discipline — including cross-functional teams with product, design, and research members.

*Human Edge connection:* All studios operate as cohorts. IS 265 simulates cross-functional teams. IS 425 requires team delivery to a real client.

## Human Edge Outcomes (Program-Specific)

In addition to the five ABET outcomes, graduates will demonstrate:

### HE-1: Directed AI Orchestration
**Direct AI agents** to accomplish complex, multi-step tasks by constructing effective Context Packs, evaluating agent output, iterating on results, and documenting the human decisions that shaped the final product.

*Evidence:* AI Audit Logs (IS 218+), Context Packs (all studios), capstone project (IS 425)

### HE-2: Judgment Under Uncertainty
**Exercise informed judgment** about AI-generated output — accepting, rejecting, or modifying based on domain knowledge, quality criteria, and professional standards — even when the AI's output appears confident and plausible.

*Evidence:* AI Audit Logs showing reasoned acceptance/rejection/modification decisions

### HE-3: Failure Ownership
**Design for failure, detect failure, and own recovery** — anticipating failure modes, building monitoring, running incident drills, and writing honest postmortems that lead to improvement.

*Evidence:* Failure mode analyses (IS 219), runbooks, incident drill documentation, capstone postmortem (IS 425)

### HE-4: Epistemic Responsibility
**Acknowledge the limitations** of data, models, and AI systems — documenting assumptions, biases, and blind spots in every data representation and AI output.

*Evidence:* Data assumptions documents (IS 331), evaluation plans (IS 390), capstone evaluation harness (IS 425)

### HE-5: Adaptive Re-Tooling
**Adapt to new tools, frameworks, and workflows** without loss of effectiveness — having practiced this through eight studio cycles where the technical domain changes every semester while human capabilities accumulate.

*Evidence:* Portfolio progression across 8 studios with 8 different technical domains
