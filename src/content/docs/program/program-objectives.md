# Program Educational Objectives (PEOs)

Program Educational Objectives describe what graduates of the BS in Enterprise AI are expected to achieve **3–5 years after graduation**. They reflect the lasting value of the degree and guide continuous program improvement.

---

## The Objectives

### PEO 1: Responsible AI Deployment
Graduates will **employ AI systems responsibly** to deliver measurable business value — designing, deploying, evaluating, and governing AI-powered solutions while maintaining ethical standards and human oversight.

### PEO 2: Informed Professional Judgment
Graduates will **exercise professional judgment** when directing AI agents, critically evaluating their output, and overriding when evidence warrants — demonstrating the discernment that distinguishes a professional from a prompt-user.

### PEO 3: Cross-Functional Leadership
Graduates will **lead cross-functional teams** through ambiguous, high-stakes problems — communicating effectively with technical and non-technical stakeholders, finding the real problem beneath the stated one, and building consensus for action.

### PEO 4: Continuous Adaptation
Graduates will **adapt continuously** to new technologies, tools, and workflows — maintaining effectiveness through the perpetual change of the AI era by applying the permanently human capabilities (inquiry, judgment, empathy, accountability) developed in the program.

### PEO 5: Accountable Ownership
Graduates will **take accountability** for the systems they build, deploy, and maintain — owning outcomes, acknowledging failures, conducting honest postmortems, and improving based on evidence.

## Who These Graduates Become

Based on [job market evidence](/bseai_degree_planning/evidence/job-market/), graduates are prepared for roles including:

- **Forward-Deployed AI Engineer** (entry $110K–$150K; mid $140K–$220K) — Prototyping AI solutions in cross-functional teams
- **AI Product Engineer** (entry $110K–$150K; mid $140K–$260K) — Building full-stack AI-powered products
- **AI Deployment Engineer** (entry $115K–$140K; mid $130K–$220K) — Ensuring safe, effective AI deployment in regulated environments
- **LLM Application Engineer** (entry $110K–$160K; mid $150K–$250K+) — Building RAG systems, eval harnesses, and production AI workflows
- **AI Product Manager** — Bridging technical AI capabilities with business needs

*Salary ranges from [canonical salary data](/bseai_degree_planning/careers/example-job-descriptions/), cross-referenced with BLS, levels.fyi, and 2025–2026 postings.*

These are not entry-level coding jobs. They are judgment-intensive roles where the **human capabilities are the differentiator** and the technical fluency is assumed.

## Assessment

PEOs are assessed through:
- Graduate surveys (1-year and 3-year post-graduation)
- Employer surveys (annual, starting Year 5)
- Job placement data (employment rate, roles, salaries)
- Industry Advisory Board feedback (annual)
